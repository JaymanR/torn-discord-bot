Privacy Policy

Effective as of 2026-11-24

This privacy policy applies to the Torn Discord Bot (the "Bot"), a Discord bot, together with any related services operated by Majestic (collectively, the "Application"). Majestic is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

When you add the Bot to a Discord server or interact with it (for example through slash commands, buttons, or direct messages), the Application may collect and process the following information:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and display name
  • The IDs and names of the Discord servers, channels, and roles in which the Bot is used
  • The content of commands and interactions you send to the Bot, and any options you supply with them
  • Your Torn API key and Torn player ID, if you choose to provide them, and the Torn game data retrieved with them
  • Technical information such as the time and date of commands, error logs, and usage statistics needed to operate and troubleshoot the Bot

The Bot does not read or store the content of messages that are not addressed to it, except to the extent Discord delivers them to the Bot as part of a command or interaction.

How Your Information Is Used

The Service Provider uses this information to provide and operate the Bot's features, to retrieve data from Torn on your behalf, to respond to your commands, to prevent abuse, and to maintain and improve the Application.

For a better experience while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your Torn API key. The information the Service Provider requests will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

Torn API Keys

A Torn API key grants access to data on your Torn account according to the access level you select when creating it. You are encouraged to create a key with the minimum access level the Bot's features require. The Service Provider uses your key only to retrieve Torn data on your behalf and does not intentionally use it for any other purpose. You can revoke or regenerate your key at any time from your Torn account settings, which immediately prevents the Bot from using it.
